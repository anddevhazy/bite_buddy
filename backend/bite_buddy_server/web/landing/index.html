Most people know they should eat better. But they don't.

Bite Buddy rewards you for eating healthy. Streaks, a pet that depends on you and battles with friends make eating well something you want to come back to every day.

How it works

Your buddy only eats what you eat, so the healthier your plate, the stronger it gets.

Adopt a buddy

Pick one of four buddies, or turn a photo of your own pet into one.

Snap your meal

An AI scanner checks it's a real, freshly taken meal (no screenshots or stock photos) and estimates its nutrition.

Watch it grow

Good meals make your buddy healthier and stronger. Skipped days make it hungry.
